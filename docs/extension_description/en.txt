MetaECF is a self-custody Ethereum wallet published by the Ethereum Community Foundation. Your keys are created and encrypted on your own device and never leave it.

What you can do with it:
- Hold ETH and tokens on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base and Polygon, or add any network by its RPC address.
- Sign in to apps and approve transactions from a clear confirmation screen that, on networks that support it, previews the balance changes before you sign.
- Use a Ledger or Trezor hardware wallet so your keys stay on the device.
- Open .eth websites by typing "eth" and a name in the address bar.

Built to protect you:
- Warnings for known phishing sites, look-alike addresses and look-alike ENS names.
- No analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. Network requests go only to the RPC endpoints you use and to the services listed in the privacy policy.

Websites find the wallet through the EIP-6963 standard, alongside any other wallets you have installed. MetaECF needs access to the pages you visit so that those sites can connect to it; the sites you visit are checked against the phishing list on your device and are never recorded or sent anywhere.

MetaECF is built from MetaMask's open-source code and is not affiliated with MetaMask or Consensys. The source code is public at https://github.com/ETHCF/ethereum-wallet
